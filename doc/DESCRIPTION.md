VIGIlance is ARCenal's employee-management application, powered by ARCenal OS.

This preview provides personal candidate dossiers, versioned FORM S 13 interest proposals, mandatory employee onboarding, HR dossiers and a Gratitude-themed interface. Administrators import welcome videos, four PDF charters and a communication QR, and create the quiz with its answer key. Refusals notify management; only acceptance of the current proposal grants the employee profile. Revisions explicitly require or waive a repeat step while retaining prior evidence.

The historical app identifier `arcenalportail` is preserved. Application data and private settings stay outside the public web root; the package includes versioned schema setup, consistent SQLite snapshots and backup/restore scripts.

YunoHost SSO authenticates users. Direct LDAP group synchronization, outgoing email, the final candidate attachment rules, official FORM S 13 PDF generation and future Dolibarr indicator export remain incomplete. Internal notifications and onboarding evidence are persisted. A fresh instance contains no demonstration people or training content. A packaging qualification does not constitute customer acceptance of these business workflows.
